ATLAS Research Tool

Video Cue Analyzer

Sends video clips to a video-capable model on OpenRouter and asks for a frame-by-frame read of observable drowning cues — body position, limb movement, breathing, and whether the person makes forward progress. No rescue narrative, just the physical cues.
Congressional App Challenge 2026

Built from my perspective as a high school student, lifeguard, and drowning-prevention researcher, ATLAS explores how video models can organize visible physical cues for human review.

No account or API key needed. Explore illustrative results and try both exports. Live analysis below requires your own OpenRouter API key.

Research prototype: ATLAS is not a validated drowning-detection or emergency-response system. Outputs may be wrong or miss distress. Never rely on it for swimmer supervision, rescue decisions, or emergency alerts.

Results
Two modes: Native video sends the actual clip. Contact sheet mode extracts labeled, evenly spaced frames locally and sends one image; it cannot capture motion between frames. Neither mode guarantees accuracy or availability.
Codec note: If your browser cannot decode a clip, try an H.264 MP4 export.
Provider note: Models, pricing, supported modalities, and rate limits can change. A 429 response means the service is rate-limited; wait or select another compatible model.
